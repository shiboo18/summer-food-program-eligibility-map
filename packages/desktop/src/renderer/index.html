Welcome to vibeCheck.

vibeCheck verifies every address in an Excel workbook against Smarty and ArcGIS, then checks USDA rural and area eligibility.

Open settings

🔒 Privacy note

Your data stays on your computer. Only the address fields are sent out — never names, phone numbers, or anything else in your spreadsheet.

Each address is sent only to the providers that check it:

  • Smarty — verifies and standardizes each address.
  • ArcGIS — finds the map location an address sits at.
  • USDA — the rural and area-eligibility maps.

    Steps

      Which USDA checks do I need?
      • USDA rural designation — whether each address is rural on USDA's summer meals map. In rural areas with no on-site meal service, summer meals can be served to go or by delivery. Results read Rural or Not Rural.
      • USDA area eligibility — whether each address is in an area where at least half the children qualify for free or reduced-price meals, which open summer meal sites need. Results read In Area — Eligible, In Area — Averaged (eligible only with State agency and USDA approval), or Not Eligible.

      Not sure? Turn both on. Each adds one column to your results.
