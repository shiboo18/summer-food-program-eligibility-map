Welcome to vibeCheck.

vibeCheck verifies and standardizes every address in an Excel workbook against Smarty. Open settings to add your Smarty credentials.

🔒 Click to see the privacy note

Your data stays on your computer. Only the address fields are sent out — never names, phone numbers, or anything else in your spreadsheet.

Each address is sent only to the provider that checks it:

  • Smarty — verifies and standardizes each address.
